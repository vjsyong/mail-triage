## Top gaps vs the commercial bar

1. **NEW — there is no keyboard surface at all on desktop.** Every triage mechanic is mouse-only: the only keydown handlers in the whole app are Escape (disarm/cancel) and Enter (send/commit) (1236, 1263, 1485, 4558); the manifest's "shortcuts" is just PWA app shortcuts (2338). Best: Fastmail's single-key model (`j`/`k`, `x`, `d`, `y`, `[`/`]`, `z` undo — P1 §2's bar is ≥5 single-key actions) and Superhuman's Cmd+K, "your master control for any action" (§2). Why it matters: on a 1440px desktop, triage speed *is* keyboard speed; this is a form-post app, one keydown layer away from the Superhuman interaction model, and it is the single highest-leverage missing pattern.
2. **NEW — snooze cannot express the one intent people actually have.** Presets are fixed durations — 24/72/168h (6696), repeated in the viewer sidebar — no "tomorrow morning", no weekend, no custom time. Best: Gmail/Spark snooze presets with clock anchoring (P1 §2), Shortwave's AI-suggested times. Why it matters: "1 day" returns mail at a random hour tomorrow; the needs-reply queue is exactly where clock-anchored snooze ("Friday 9am") does its work.
3. **persisting — search still cannot become a rule or a saved view.** The messages search row is Search + "More filters" only; the rules page offers only "New rule". Best: Fastmail — any search result becomes a rule; Gmail — build a filter from criteria and test the search before committing (P2 §2, refs 11–12); Shortwave's custom-query Splits (P1 §1). Why it matters: it's the loop that makes a triage app quieter over time, and the evidence is already on screen when the search is run.
4. **persisting — undo lives a page away from the mutation.** Machine-filing undo exists (engine.py:1785, `undo_log` store.py:372) but surfaces only as the dashboard's recent-filings card; approvals carry no undo. Best: Gemini gates agentic actions with confirm plus a 60s undo right where you confirmed (P1 §5). Why it matters: approve-then-regret is the one real risk of agentic filing; reversal that requires finding the change elsewhere makes every approval heavier.
5. **persisting — the needs-reply queue never ages, nudges, or resurfaces.** "10 need a reply" is a static counter + filter; snooze is manual; repo-wide there are no reminder/resurface strings outside demo/benchmark data. Best: Superhuman Auto Reminders (resurface unanswered after 1–7 days), HEY Bubble Up (P1 §2). Why it matters: needs-reply is the app's core promise — a queue that never escalates becomes wallpaper.
