# Desktop critique — 2026-10-01 (1440×900, 8 screens)

Critic pass vs `docs/ux-benchmarks.md` (cited by part/section), plus `docs/research-lists.md` and `docs/research-forms.md` for page-specific bars. Every screen inspected once; 3 zoom crops; vision claims double-checked against the live DOM (127.0.0.1:8097) where they mattered. Deliberate invariants (square corners, Vercel-light, Geist, black logs, blue links) not flagged. Trap re-verified clean: the viewer "…receive a da" body cut is the known false positive — DOM holds the full sentence ("…a daily report of some notifications from your Canvas account…") and `.emailbody` is `overflow-x:auto` by design (wide newsletter HTML scrolls; app.py:3723) — not reported as a defect.

## Verdicts

- **dashboard — par vs Vercel/Linear dashboard bar (Part 2 §1).** Bar: hero stat answers one question and links to the filtered list it summarizes; status card names state + action; "recent" data timestamped. Ours: "934 need a reply" *and* its "view →" both link to `/messages?f=needs_reply`; the status strip names state and cadence (checked 42s ago · next in ~47s · LLM calls 0/40); recent mail is timestamped. Slips below par on the table itself (WHEN column collapses, nit 1) and the hero card carries pipeline vanity next to the exceptions.
- **messages — worse vs Gmail/Superhuman list bar (Part 1 §1).** Their bar: sender + subject + snippet + time with an *unmistakable unread affordance*; ≥2 scoping axes beyond "All"; read items recede; dominating senders collapse. Ours has the row anatomy, counted filters (All 3559 / Needs reply 934 / Sorted 2479 …), "page 1 of 36" and a bulk bar — but the DOM shows every row at font-weight 400, no unread dot/background, no read/unread concept in the template at all, and the axes are pipeline states, not sender/topic; two adjacent `cloud_noreply@tencent.com` rows stand alone where HEY/Shortwave bundle. Furthest screen from the bar.
- **viewer — worse vs Fastmail/Gmail reading-pane bar (Part 1 §3).** Bar: next/prev + archive-and-advance + reply/reply-all/forward; the pane carries its own actions. Ours: one-way "← Messages"; the only forms are `/draft` ("Draft with LLM"), `/classify`, `/tag` — no next/prev, no archive-and-advance, no manual move/file, no plain reply surface (replies exist only as LLM drafts into Drafts). Credit where due: nothing is sent automatically and the AI output lands as a draft, matching the "editable preview" half of the bar.
- **assistant — better vs Shortwave/Gemini AI-surfacing bar (Part 1 §5).** Bar: chat that can search/act across the mailbox with visible tool actions; every mutation gated; audit trust. Ours: streamed thinking/tool steps, saved chat history, "proposes rules you approve with one click", and a standing permissions footer (`may do directly: classify, flag, tag, move, create_folder, …; disabled: delete, send`) — a stronger written guarantee than Gemini's confirm+undo (Part 1 §5c). Weak: footer is dev-speak; no per-message copy/regenerate (not required by the bar — noted, not counted).
- **rules — par vs Outlook/Gmail/Zapier rule-builder bar (Part 2 §2).** Bar: ordered editable conditions/actions, non-destructive toggle-off, dry-run before commit, guarded reorder, teaching empty state. Ours: numbered order with "first match wins" stated in the page description, named guard rules ("keep in place"), "Test against last 200 messages" dry-run, per-row disable, button reorder (WCAG 2.5.7-correct — research-lists §5 explicitly prefers buttons over drag). Only open question: where the dry-run results render (nit 8).
- **classifiers — par on function, worse on scan vs this repo's own brief + M3 Lists (research-lists §/classifiers).** The brief allows the 8-column table but says move `kind`/`matches on` into row-detail "if it feels wide"; M3 Lists wants short scannable items. Ours does the important part right — "WEAK" badge and "1 REMOVED" are text, not colour-only — but the MATCHES ON cells dump raw decision-list rules into a wall of mono fragments (nit 9).
- **settings — par vs settings bar (Part 2 §5; research-forms §1, Eleken).** Bar: task-named sections, per-scope saves with feedback, every setting shows its current value, helper text per field. Ours: task groups + sub-nav, per-card "Save general" / "Save LLM endpoint" (the brief's own partial-save contract), populated values, "Blank fields fall back to the env file." Only sliver: the time-offset unit lives in helper text (nit 10).
- **accounts — par vs connected-account pattern (research-lists §/accounts; Shopify/Stripe via ui-redesign).** Bar: show integration status plainly, set risky actions apart, destructive last. Ours: proxy strip with state + Restart/Proxy log, account card with `AUTHORISED`/`READING` badges and token expiry, status-driven Re-authorise, Edit, and a More menu holding destructive items; raw pid/port/loopback detail is *deliberately kept* on desktop per the brief — not flagged.

## Top gaps vs the commercial bar

1. **No unread/read model in the list.** Best: Gmail ("unmistakable unread affordance", Part 1 §1) and Spark, whose read mail drops into a Seen section. Our DOM has no "seen/unread" concept in the messages template — rows render identically (weight 400), while the dashboard celebrates "70% of 3559 seen". For a triage app, "what is new vs what I already handled" is the first scan; without it the 934-item Needs-reply queue is a wall of identical rows.
2. **No reply queue / advance mechanics.** Best: HEY's Reply Later + Focus & Reply ("reply one after another"), Gmail auto-advance, Fastmail's `]` archive-and-next. The app computes the exact worklist (Needs reply 934) and then opens a viewer with no next/prev and no advance-after-action — every reply costs a round-trip through the list. At 934 items this is the difference between a queue and a browse.
3. **No snooze / resurface / reminders.** Best: Superhuman Auto Reminders (resurface in 1–7 days), Spark snooze presets, HEY Bubble Up. Mail here can only be moved or flagged — follow-ups have no "bring this back Tuesday" mechanism (the `Awaiting LLM` state is pipeline, not deferral).
4. **No keyboard layer or command palette on desktop.** Best: Superhuman Cmd/Ctrl+K as master control with hover-learned shortcuts; Fastmail's single-key model (j/k, x, d, y, `]`, `z` = undo of last ~10 actions). The bar asks ≥5 single-key actions + global undo; this desktop surface — the one with a physical keyboard — exposes no shortcut model and no undo concept at all.
5. **Scoping axes are pipeline states, not triage contexts — and no sender bundling.** Best: Gmail labels, Superhuman Split Inboxes, Shortwave Splits + Bundles, HEY's one-row collapse of a dominating sender. Our filters (Awaiting LLM / Needs reply / Sorted / Tagged / Errors) are excellent ops telemetry and the counts are real, but there is no axis by sender or topic, and a noisy sender stacks rows instead of collapsing to one.

## Nits

1. **Dashboard — recent-mail "WHEN" column.** Dates wrap to 3–4 lines (DOM: 56px cell, `overflow-wrap:anywhere`, 4 line boxes for "10-01 18:30"; both crop passes agree). Source: Part 2 §1 (dashboard scannability; timestamps on recent data). **major.** Fix: `white-space:nowrap` + min-width on the time cell.
2. **Dashboard — hero card.** "sorted 2479 (70%)" and "LLM classified 3530 (99%)" are pipeline metrics sitting beside the actionable 934; "KPI tiles answering no user question" is the named anti-pattern (Part 2 §1, [23]). **minor.** Fix: demote to one sub-line under the exceptions ("2479 sorted · 3530 classified").
3. **Dashboard — search-index duplicated.** The same figures ("5239 chunks · folders 6/7") appear in the status strip and again in the Search-index card. Source: Part 2 §1 (cards = single-topic groupings; state + action). **minor.** Fix: strip says "ready · no errors"; the numbers live once, in the card with its Index now/Rebuild actions.
4. **Messages — TAG column reserved but empty.** DOM confirms a TAG header with every sampled row empty (8/8). Source: M3 Lists via research-lists §7 (columns carry a fact; short scannable items). **minor.** Fix: hide the column when the current filter yields no tags.
5. **Messages — status vocabulary without a legend.** Five variants in play (SORTED, FLOW, CLASSIFIED, LLM → FOLDER, ASSISTANT → FOLDER); the arrow/agent notation is opaque on first read. Source: Part 1 §1 (row anatomy) + Part 2 §1 (status states state plainly). **minor.** Fix: one-line legend in the page description or `title` tooltips.
6. **Viewer — no manual file/move control on the open message.** Forms on the page: draft / classify / tag only; filing goes through "Classify with LLM". Source: Part 1 §2 (move-to-label is a standard triage action; the pane should carry its own actions, §3). **major.** Fix: add a Move-to dropdown to the Actions card.
7. **Viewer — Message-ID renders as a wrapped blob.** `.mono` + `overflow-wrap:anywhere` breaks the ~80-char id over ~8 lines with no copy affordance. Source: Part 2 §3 ("monospace IDs with copy affordances"). **minor.** Fix: clamp to 2 lines + copy button.
8. **Rules — dry-run result surface not visible in this state.** "Test against last 200 messages" exists (bar met), but nothing on the page shows what matched. Source: Part 2 §2 (Gmail verify-before-commit; Zapier's Test tab shows Data in/out). **minor** (results may render on click — phrased as uncertainty). Fix: inline results card under the button.
9. **Classifiers — MATCHES ON column.** Full decision-list dumps (`body contains 'sean' → __other__ (100%, n=61); …`) are unreadable at a glance; the repo brief itself says row-detail if the table feels wide. Source: research-lists §/classifiers; Pencil & Paper row-detail rule. **minor.** Fix: summary cell ("61 rules · min conf 0.80") + expandable detail.
10. **Settings — Time display offset.** Value "8.0" carries its unit only in helper text ("Hours from UTC"). Source: [NNg-forms] explain formatting requirements / GOV.UK hint placement (research-forms §0). **nit.** Fix: label "Time offset (hours)".
11. **Messages — no visible thread/conversation affordance.** Rows read as single messages; no thread count or chevron is shown. Source: Part 1 §1 ("threads grouped by default"). **minor** (phrased as uncertainty — grouping may exist but present unlabelled). Fix: show "N messages" on grouped rows.

## Summary

- No blockers: all 8 desktop screens render coherently and the Vercel vocabulary holds at 1440.
- The gap to commercial products is triage mechanics, not looks: no unread model, no reply queue, no snooze, no keyboard/undo, pipeline-only scoping — all list-level and theme-agnostic to fix.
- Best-in-class within the app: the assistant's permissions footer + gated mutations, which beat Gemini's pattern on paper; dashboard hero → filtered-list linking meets the bar.
- Worst surface: messages vs Gmail/Superhuman on unread + grouping; only screen that would disappoint a Superhuman user on first contact.
- Fix-order suggestion: WHEN column wrap (major) and viewer manual-move (major), then the five product gaps in the order listed.
- One prior-pass trap re-verified clean (viewer body "da…" cut = false positive; DOM holds the full sentence, overflow-x:auto by design).
