## Top gaps vs the commercial bar

1. **No keyboard layer and no command palette.** Gap: repo-verified — the only global key handlers are Escape (disarm, close scrim) and Enter in textareas/comboboxes; no palette, no j/k. Who does it best: Superhuman (Cmd/Ctrl+K = "master control for any action"; shortcuts shown inline) and Fastmail (j/k/x/d/y/[ ]/z — undo up to 10 actions) [§2]. Why it matters: §2's bar is ≥5 single-key actions; an app that auto-sorts 99% of mail is a throughput tool, and the core loop (list → read → dispose → next) stays mouse-speed.
2. **The "Needs reply" loop's last mile happens outside the app.** Gap: drafts land in Drafts or the clipboard ("Review and send from your mail client") and there is no forward/reply-all at all — so the one queue the dashboard promotes ends in a context switch to another client. Who does it best: HEY's Focus & Reply ("reply one after another" until the pile empties) and Shortwave/Gmail, where drafting and sending are one surface. Why it matters: this app's headline concept is the needs-reply queue; a queue you must finish elsewhere keeps the triage app as a router, not the workplace. (Deliberate safety tradeoff — acknowledged — but still the biggest functional delta vs the §3 bar.)
3. **Undo reach stops at filings.** Gap: dashboard keeps per-filing Undo + "Undo puts one back and keeps automation off it", and the viewer has file-undo — but nothing visible in these shots covers bulk or assistant-driven mutations, and the assistant page shows approvals ("Asks first", pending approvals) without a visible undo for what was already approved and done. Who does it best: Fastmail's 10-action undo stack and Gmail Gemini's 60s undo on confirmed agentic actions [§2, §5]. Why it matters: §5(c) makes confirm + undo the trust floor for mail-mutating agents; reachable undo is what keeps "never deletes mail" credible.
4. **Classifier trust metrics aren't on the classifier surface.** Gap: "Held-out label agreement", accuracy, precision/recall render on the Learning page's cards, not on the Classifiers table where each model is toggled or edited (repo-verified). Who does it best: any supervised-model console puts the error rate next to the enable switch; the doc's §2/§8 tooling bar wants thresholds and states to be deliberate and legible. Why it matters: you are asked to let these fast-paths "answer before the AI sees it" while the surface hides their measured error; "weak" without a number is a mood, not a fact.
5. **Read/unread state is invisible in the list.** Gap: no unread weight, dot, or filter in the message table (crop-verified + repo), and the "Seen 3610" phrasing is the only read-state vocabulary in the app. Who does it best: every peer — Gmail/Spark/Shortwave all make unread unmistakable per §1's bar. Why it matters: triage means "what still needs me"; automation badges (SORTED / LLM→FOLDER / CLASSIFIED) answer "what did the system do", not "what haven't I looked at", so an already-read message and a never-seen one scan identically.

## Nits

1. **minor** — Assistant — context rail: chat rows are title-only, ellipsized, with near-duplicate titles indistinguishable ("Find me messages related to…" twice; two rule chats). §8 craft (states deliberate) — add a last-message snippet + full-title tooltip, and date beyond the day group.
2. **minor** — Assistant — landing behavior: the page opens on an empty state while 10 saved chats exist; no chat is selected on arrival. §8 (empty states are for "nothing here", not "nothing chosen") — open the most recent chat by default, or add "Continue last chat →".
3. **minor** — Messages — TAG column: empty on every visible row yet consumes width (§1 density) — auto-hide the column when a page has no tags.
4. **minor** — Viewer — already-filed mail: no file/move/un-file control renders because `can_file` is false once a move happened (repo-verified) — §3 bar wants the reading pane to carry its own actions — always offer "Move to folder…" with a destination picker.
5. **minor** — Viewer — workbench hierarchy: "Draft reply" is the solid-black primary even on an auto-filed newsletter with no reply need; §8 (match visual weight to the action's meaning) — demote Draft reply to secondary when the LLM says no reply is needed.
6. **minor** — Settings — sub-nav: anchor links without scroll-spy, so the "current" item and the viewport drift apart on a long page (Part 2 §5 grouping) — add scroll-spy active state (or split into pages).
7. **minor** — Settings/footer + Classes page footers — "app data in /data", "no permanent deletion" are operator strings repeated on every page (Part 2 §5: settings hold infrequent config; §8 craft) — move to the Advanced disclosure, keep one user-language line.
8. **minor** — Classifiers — vocabulary: "WEAK", "min conf 0.80", "-1 REMOVED", "__other__" are undefined on the page that shows them (§3 text-equivalents; §8 deliberate thresholds) — one-line legend + tooltips; print confidence as "≥80%" and rename `__other__` to "everything else".
9. **nit** — Classifiers — MATCHES ON: `body contains 'departmen'` reads as a clipped token; it may be intentional prefix-matching (uncertain — a still cannot settle whether the stored token or the display is truncated) — if display, add a title expansion; if stored, that's worth a look.
10. **nit** — Dashboard — statistics card: "% of 3610 seen" uses the app's own "seen" noun unglossed (Part 2 §1) — say "of 3,610 messages checked".
11. **nit** — Accounts — card: sync scope and history depth are invisible (collapsed "Setup details"), and AUTHORISED has no adjacent Test affordance (§6 "state what is available") — surface "reads last N days" on the card + add Test connection.
12. **nit** — Messages — toolbar: "More filters" never shows what's applied when collapsed (a chip/count would) (§2 presets beat menus; §8 states) — show an applied-filter count on the summary.

## Summary

1. Two of yesterday's three table-stakes gaps are closed: the list now has search (+ advanced filters), and the viewer now has a real reply path (draft → review → save, never auto-send).
2. Rules, Accounts and Settings stay at or above commercial par; the assistant is par but its rail and landing behavior undersell the content behind it.
3. Remaining product gaps, ranked: no keyboard layer/palette (Superhuman/Fastmail), the needs-reply last mile exits the app (no send, no forward), undo reach stops short of agent actions, classifier error rates missing where classifiers are managed, and no read/unread model in the list.
4. The viewer's fixed reply path is now the model citizen of §3/§4; its remaining hole is already-filed mail, which offers no move/un-file.
5. Trust instrumentation remains the app's best habit — timestamps, tooltips, "teaches the models; does not move mail", retention copy — and is now let down mainly by missing *numbers* (classifier accuracy, sync scope) rather than wrong words.
